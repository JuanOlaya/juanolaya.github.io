German Diphthongs (Doppellaute)

German Umlauts (ä, ö, ü)

Umlauts are modified vowels that create different sounds and meanings.
🇪🇸 Las diéresis son vocales modificadas que crean sonidos y significados diferentes.

Umlaut IPA Sound How to Pronounce German Examples English Approximation Practice Tips

German ß (Eszett/Sharp S)

The German ß represents a sharp "s" sound and follows specific rules.
🇪🇸 La ß alemana representa un sonido "s" agudo y sigue reglas específicas.

Rule When to Use ß German Examples English Translation Alternative (ss) Memory Tip
After Long Vowels Long vowel + ß Straße (street)
groß (big)		 street, big Strasse, gross Long sound = ß
After Diphthongs Diphthong + ß weiß (white)
heiß (hot)		 white, hot weiss, heiss Diphthong = ß
Never After Short Vowels Use "ss" instead dass (that)
Wasser (water)		 that, water Always ss Short sound = ss
Never at Word Beginning ß never starts words Sonne (sun)
sein (to be)		 sun, to be Always s at start ß = middle/end only
