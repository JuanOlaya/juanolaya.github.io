Tipps zum Geschlecht der Substantive
Gender Article Common Patterns / Endings Examples
🔴 Masculine der - Days, months, seasons
- Points of the compass
- Nouns ending in -er, -en, -ig, -ling
- Male people or animals		 der Montag (Monday), der Sommer (summer), der Westen (west), der Lehrer (teacher), der König (king), der Mann (man), der Hund (dog)
🔵 Feminine die - Nouns ending in -e (often)
- Endings: -ung, -heit, -keit, -schaft, -ion, -tät, -ik
- Female people or animals		 die Lampe (lamp), die Zeitung (newspaper), die Freiheit (freedom), die Universität (university), die Frau (woman), die Katze (cat)
⚫ Neuter das - Endings: -chen, -lein (diminutives)
- Infinitives used as nouns
- Most English loanwords
- Metals & chemical elements		 das Mädchen (girl), das Büchlein (little book), das Essen (food), das Handy (cell phone), das Internet, das Gold (gold)
